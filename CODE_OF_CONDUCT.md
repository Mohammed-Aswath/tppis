# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to Mohammed Aswath M at mohammed.aswath07@gmail.com or through the [repository issue tracker](https://github.com/Mohammed-Aswath/tppis/issues). Maintainers will review and respond as promptly as they can.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may result in a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
